Interactive Simulators

Each page runs entirely in your browser. Tap parts and parameters to explore idealized teaching models, their equations and assumptions.

Optional: Download the MATLAB DC Motor Designer v2.3 (ZIP) for a separate interactive MATLAB implementation, including the conventional torque-speed graph. MATLAB is not required for the browser simulations. The updated MATLAB plot has not yet been run in MATLAB.

Educational use only. Motor outputs are calculated predictions, not verified datasheet values, rated limits, or motor selection advice. Bus diagrams are conceptual, not tested electrical schematics or code. The motor examples are not manufacturer-calibrated. Review each page's model assumptions and the model and validation notes before drawing quantitative or hardware-related conclusions.
DC Motor DesignerChange winding, geometry, magnets, gearing and load; see the classic torque-versus-speed curve, motor constants, other performance curves and step response. Coreless DC Motor DesignerIdealized ironless architecture: magnet, winding shell and skew set the constants, inertia, classic torque-speed graph and heating. Brushless DC Motor DesignerIdealized slotless brushless architecture: poles, yoke iron loss, the conventional torque-speed graph, and commutation styles. I²C Bus InspectorTwo Minimas and a 5 V logic-compatible LSM6DS3TR-C IMU breakout on I²C, one clock edge at a time. SPI Bus InspectorThe same 5 V-compatible IMU breakout and two Minimas on SPI: chip selects, full duplex and contention. UART / Serial Baud-Rate InspectorChange the transmitter and receiver baud rates and watch the same 8N1 frame decode differently when sample clocks disagree.
