Interactive Simulators

Six interactive teaching tools for motor behavior and digital communication. Run any page in your browser.

Optional MATLAB demo: Download DC Motor Designer v2.6.1 (ZIP). Browser simulators do not require MATLAB.

Educational models only. Motor values are predictions, not ratings. Bus waveforms are illustrations, not validated circuits. See model notes before applying results to hardware.
DC Motor DesignerAdjust winding, magnets, gearing, and load; compare torque-speed curves and transients. Coreless DC Motor DesignerExplore ironless winding geometry, inertia, performance, and heating. Brushless DC Motor DesignerExplore poles, winding geometry, losses, and commutation illustrations. I²C Bus InspectorExplore addressing, ACK/NACK, and pull-up timing. SPI Bus InspectorExplore chip select, clock timing, and full-duplex transfers. UART / Serial Baud-Rate InspectorExplore baud mismatch, sampling, and framing errors.
